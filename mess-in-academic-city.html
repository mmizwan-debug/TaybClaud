Mess in Academic City — FAQ

Is there a mess for students in Academic City?

Yes. Tayb Meals delivers monthly mess food and North Indian tiffin meals to students and staff in Dubai Academic City, Monday to Saturday. Plans are AED 199 per month for 1 meal a day, AED 359 for 2 meals and AED 459 for 3 meals.

What time is food delivered in Academic City?

In Academic City, breakfast and lunch are delivered together before about 7:00 AM, and dinner before about 7:00 PM. Times are approximate.

Can I stop if the food doesn’t suit me?

Yes. With the Starter Plan, payment is due on the 2nd day of delivery. If you stop, you pay only for the meals you received.

Do you deliver on Sunday in Academic City?
