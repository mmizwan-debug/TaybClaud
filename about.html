About Tayb Meals

Homestyle Kerala & Tamil Nadu mess food, all across Dubai.

Tayb Meals is a monthly mess food and tiffin service in Dubai. We cook halal, homestyle Kerala, Tamil Nadu / South Indian and North Indian meals and deliver breakfast, lunch and dinner to homes and offices all across Dubai, Monday to Saturday, with an optional Sunday add-on. Monthly plans start at AED 199 for one meal a day.

Why Tayb Meals started

Life in Dubai is busy, and cooking every day isn’t always possible — especially after a long day at work or in a shared flat. Tayb Meals was created to make everyday eating simple, affordable and convenient: real homestyle food, the kind you grew up with, delivered to your door every day. Our promise is in our tagline: Pure. Good. Delivered.

Food that tastes like home

We have more than 300 dishes, and the menu changes every week, so you are not eating the same thing again and again.

  • Kerala: matta rice meals, Kerala-style fish and chicken curries, parotta with beef or chicken, puttu, idiyappam and Kasargod-style choru.
  • Tamil Nadu & South Indian: idly, dosa, pongal, sambar rice, rasam, kuzhambu, curd rice, lemon rice and tamarind rice, with Tamil Nadu-style lunches and dinners.
  • North Indian & more: North Indian meals and other dishes for variety, plus vegetarian options.

See this week’s dishes in our Kerala menu, South Indian menu or the full E-Menu Book. Malayali? See our Kerala mess in Dubai page.

Who we cook for

Malayali and Tamil families, bachelors, roommates in shared apartments and office teams who miss home food and want a reliable daily mess in Dubai — from Jebel Ali, Al Quoz and DIP to Karama, Bur Dubai, Deira, Al Qusais, Al Nahda and International City.
