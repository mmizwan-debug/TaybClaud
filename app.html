Is Tayb Meals a monthly mess / tiffin service?

Yes. Tayb Meals is a monthly mess and tiffin service in Dubai. Choose a monthly meal plan from AED 199 per month and get homestyle breakfast, lunch and dinner delivered Monday to Saturday (Sunday optional) to your home or office.

Do you serve Kerala food?

Yes. Our menu has a dedicated Kerala selection, including matta rice meals, Kerala-style fish and chicken curries, parotta with beef or chicken, puttu, idiyappam and Kasargod-style choru, alongside South Indian and North Indian dishes. Use the Kerala filter in our E-Menu Book to see them.

Do you serve South Indian food?

Yes. Besides our Kerala selection, our menu has South Indian meals such as sambar rice, curd rice, lemon rice, pongal and dosa, with Tamil Nadu-style lunches and dinners. Use the South Indian filter in our E-Menu Book to see them.

Can I change my menu?

Yes. You can change your menu anytime or update it weekly to explore more meal options from our menu.

Can I pause deliveries?

Yes. You can pause or hold your deliveries by contacting our support team on WhatsApp.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can continue, change your menu, or cancel anytime with 1 working day’s notice. You only pay for the meals you have received and applicable fees. Any eligible refund will be processed at the end of your service period.

How does the Starter Plan work?

Enjoy your first 2 days of meals with complete peace of mind — payment is due on the 2nd day of delivery.

Satisfied? Continue with your monthly prepaid fee on the 2nd day of delivery. Not satisfied? Cancel and pay only for the meals you've received.

Per meal cost is approximately AED 5–8. Need to change or cancel? Just give us 1 working day's notice.

When should I pay my monthly prepaid amount?

Your monthly prepaid amount is due on the 2nd day of delivery.

We accept:

  • Bank Transfer
  • Aani
  • Credit Card
  • Debit Card
  • Tabby
  • Tamara
  • CDM (Cash Deposit Machine)

Cash payments and card machine payments are not available.

Where do you deliver?

We deliver fresh home-style meals to most areas across Dubai.

What is the cost of the Starter Plan?

Meals typically cost approximately AED 5–8 each during the Starter Plan — you only pay for what you've received, with payment due on the 2nd day of delivery. If you continue, you'll move to your monthly prepaid fee; if not, you can cancel with 1 working day's notice.

What are your service days?

Our regular service days are Monday to Saturday. Sunday is available as an optional add-on service.

What is the Sunday add-on cost?

Lunch only: AED 30/month
Lunch + Dinner: AED 50/month
Breakfast is not available on Sundays.

Do you offer fixed menus?

Yes. You can choose either:

  • Custom Menu – Select your preferred meals.
  • Fixed Menu – We select meals for you based on our weekly menu.

You can switch between them anytime.

How often is the menu updated?

Our menu is updated regularly, and customers can review and update their meal selections weekly.

Can I change my delivery address?

Yes. Delivery address changes can be requested through our support team, subject to service availability and route coverage.

Do you deliver to homes and offices?

Yes. We deliver to both homes and offices across most areas of Dubai.
