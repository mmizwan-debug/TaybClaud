Kerala Mess in Dubai

Kerala mess food, delivered all across Dubai.

Tayb Meals is a monthly mess in Dubai for Malayalis who miss nadan food. Halal, homestyle Kerala breakfast, lunch and dinner — matta rice meals, puttu, idiyappam, parotta and more — delivered to your home or office Monday to Saturday. Monthly plans start at AED 199 for one meal a day.

Kerala dishes on our weekly menu

We have more than 300 dishes and the menu changes every week. Kerala favourites include:

  • Breakfast: puttu (Kerala style breakfast combo), idiyappam, parotta, Kerala dosa platter, uppumavu.
  • Lunch: matta fish meals, matta chicken meals, the Kerala veg meal with matta rice and sambar, Kasargod palli beef choru, Malabar and Kannur chicken biriyani, neychoru.
  • Dinner: idiyappam or parotta with Kerala chicken stew, nadan chicken curry with neychoru, special Kasargod chicken choru.

See this week’s dishes in the Kerala menu. Tamil Nadu and other South Indian meals are in the South Indian menu (see also Tamil mess in Dubai), and North Indian dishes are there too if you want variety.

Kerala mess FAQ

Is there a Kerala mess in Dubai that delivers?

Yes. Tayb Meals is a monthly mess food service in Dubai with a dedicated Kerala menu. We deliver homestyle Kerala breakfast, lunch and dinner to homes and offices all across Dubai, Monday to Saturday, with an optional Sunday add-on.

How much does a Kerala mess cost in Dubai?

Tayb Meals monthly plans are AED 199 per month for 1 meal a day, AED 359 for 2 meals a day and AED 459 for 3 meals a day. Sunday is an optional add-on: AED 30 for lunch only or AED 50 for lunch and dinner.

What Kerala dishes are on the menu?

The weekly menu includes matta rice fish and chicken meals, a Kerala veg meal with sambar, puttu, idiyappam with Kerala chicken stew, parotta, Kerala dosa platter, nadan chicken curry with neychoru, Kasargod palli beef choru and Malabar chicken biriyani. The menu changes every week.

Is the food halal?

Yes. All Tayb Meals food is halal.

Do you have Kerala veg meals?

Yes. Veg options include the Kerala veg meal with matta rice and sambar, puttu, idiyappam, Kerala dosa platter and uppumavu. Use the Veg Only switch in our E-Menu Book.
