Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can continue, change your menu, or cancel anytime with 1 working day’s notice. You only pay for the meals you have received and applicable fees. Any eligible refund will be processed at the end of your service period.

How does the Starter Plan work?

The Starter Plan lets you try Tayb Meals with complete peace of mind.

If you are satisfied, you can continue with a monthly prepaid plan.

If not, you may change your menu or cancel anytime and pay only for the meals you have received. Meals typically cost around AED 5–7 per meal, depending on your plan.

Simply inform us 1 working day in advance.

When should I pay my monthly prepaid amount?

Your monthly prepaid amount is due on the 3rd day of delivery.

We accept:

  • Bank Transfer
  • Aani
  • Credit Card
  • Debit Card
  • Tabby
  • Tamara
  • CDM (Cash Deposit Machine)

Cash payments and card machine payments are not available.

Where do you deliver?

We deliver fresh home-style meals to most areas across Dubai.

Can I change my delivery address?

Yes. Delivery address changes can be requested through our support team, subject to service availability and route coverage.
