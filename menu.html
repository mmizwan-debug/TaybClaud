Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can continue, change your menu, or cancel anytime with 1 working day’s notice. You only pay for the meals you have received and applicable fees. Any eligible refund will be processed at the end of your service period.

How does the Starter Plan work?

Enjoy your first 2 days of meals with complete peace of mind — payment is due on the 2nd day of delivery.

Satisfied? Continue with your monthly prepaid fee on the 2nd day of delivery. Not satisfied? Cancel and pay only for the meals you've received.

Per meal cost is approximately AED 5–8. Need to change or cancel? Just give us 1 working day's notice.

When should I pay my monthly prepaid amount?

Your monthly prepaid amount is due on the 2nd day of delivery.

We accept:

  • Bank Transfer
  • Aani
  • Credit Card
  • Debit Card
  • Tabby
  • Tamara
  • CDM (Cash Deposit Machine)

Cash payments and card machine payments are not available.

Where do you deliver?

We deliver fresh home-style meals all across Dubai — from Jebel Ali and Dubai Marina to Deira, Al Qusais and International City. Pick your area above to see your delivery time.

Do you have veg meals?

Yes. Every week’s menu has veg meals, such as idly, dosa, pongal sambar, idiyappam, curd rice, sambar with rice and the Kerala veg meal with matta rice, plus North Indian veg dishes like chappathi with chole curry. Turn on “Veg Only” using the switch at the top of this menu to see only veg dishes.

Is this a Kerala or Tamil mess menu?

Both. This is our weekly mess food menu for Dubai, with Kerala dishes like puttu, idiyappam, parotta and matta rice meals, and Tamil Nadu dishes like idly, dosa, pongal, sambar, rasam and kuzhambu, plus North Indian meals. Plans start from AED 199 a month, delivered all across Dubai. Read more about our Kerala mess in Dubai and our Tamil mess in Dubai.

Can I change my delivery address?

Yes. Delivery address changes can be requested through our support team, subject to service availability and route coverage.
