Tamil Mess in Dubai

Tamil mess food, delivered all across Dubai.

Tayb Meals is a monthly mess in Dubai for Tamil families, bachelors and office workers who miss home food. Tamil Nadu dishes on our weekly menu

We have more than 300 dishes and the menu changes every week. Tamil favourites include:

  • Breakfast: idly set with sambar, pongal sambar with gothsu, dosa, upma, poori.

  • Dinner: dosa with Madurai-style chicken salna, parotta with chicken salna, kuzhi paniyaram, idly kuruma, rice with paruppu rasam and chicken chukka, Ambur-style chicken biryani.
