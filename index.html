Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change my menu?

Yes, existing customers can easily change their menu selections via our Menu Change form.

Can I pause deliveries?

Yes, you can hold deliveries or pause your subscription by contacting our general support team on WhatsApp.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can continue, change your menu, or cancel anytime with 1 working day’s notice. You only pay for the meals you have received. Any eligible refund will be processed at the end of your planned service period.

How does the trial work?

If you're satisfied after your trial, you can continue by paying your monthly prepaid amount. If not, you can change your menu, or cancel anytime — just inform us 1 day in advance.

Do I need to pay anything to start my order?

No, you don't need to pay anything to start your delivery.

Do I need to pay an advance amount?

No advance is required. You can pay by the 3rd day of your delivery, at the latest.

When should I pay my monthly prepaid amount?

Your monthly prepaid amount is due by the 3rd day of delivery. You can pay via bank transfer, payment link, or CDM (Cash Deposit Machine).

Where do you deliver?

We deliver our fresh homestyle meals across Dubai.

What is the cost of the 2-Day Starter Plan?

The 2-Day Starter Plan lets you try Tayb Meals with complete peace of mind. If you're satisfied, you can continue with a monthly prepaid plan. If not, you may change your menu or cancel anytime and pay only for the meals you have received. Our meals typically cost around AED 5–7 per meal.

What are your service days?

Our regular service days are Monday to Saturday. Sunday is available as an optional add-on service.

What is the Sunday add-on cost?

Lunch only: AED 30/month
Lunch + Dinner: AED 50/month
Breakfast is not available on Sundays.
